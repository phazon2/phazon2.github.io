## Why this and not the YouTube channel

93.4% of that 61-video playlist teaches one thing: build your own YouTube
channel. That path needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours before the
first dollar, and the bar doubles to 8,000 hours for anyone applying after
1 Feb 2027. Months, minimum.

Scriptwriting pays in weeks, needs no audience, and needs no capital. Video 058
of the corpus states $200-2,000 per script starting out. Treat that as the
creator's own STATED figure, not a forecast.

## Your unfair advantage

You can hand a creator an analysis of their own back catalogue — hooks,
pacing, retention devices, the gap in their coverage — produced by tooling you
own. Everyone else pastes a video into a chatbot. The prerequisite video 058
names is "understanding of a specific YouTube content niche", and that is the
one thing you demonstrably now have.
