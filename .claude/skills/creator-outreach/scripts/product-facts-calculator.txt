# Job Pricing & Quoting Calculator — the only claims an email may make
#
# Same rule as the bookkeeping product: if it is not on this list, it does not
# go in an email. Cycle 02 produced a draft claiming "twelve monthly input
# tabs" and a "printable customer statement view", neither of which existed.

FILE
- One .xlsx workbook, five sheets. No macros.
- Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers and LibreOffice.
- Unit-agnostic: square metres, square feet, acres, per window, per visit,
  per hour or per day — the rate card row names its own unit.
- Currency-agnostic: the symbol is set in the number formats at build time.

SHEET 1 — Start here
- Four numbered steps and two warnings. Nothing to fill in.

SHEET 2 — My Costs
- Eight editable fixed-cost lines (vehicle, equipment, insurance, phone and
  software, advertising, premises, accountant, other).
- A line for what the owner wants to earn in a year, before tax.
- Weeks per year, days per week, billable hours per day.
- Returns BREAK-EVEN HOURLY RATE = (fixed costs + owner's pay) / billable hours.

SHEET 3 — Rate Card
- 17 pre-filled rows, all editable, covering block paving, slab and sandstone
  patios, tarmac, concrete, decking, tile roof soft wash, hand moss removal,
  render, gutters, fascia, domestic windows, maintained and overgrown mowing,
  brush cutting per acre, leaf clearance, and a machine day rate.
- Each row: rate per unit, units done per hour, and the effective hourly rate
  that comes out of those two.

SHEET 4 — Quote Builder
- One job. Takes work type (copied from the rate card), measurement,
  three multipliers (access, condition, unknowns), chemicals and consumables,
  disposal fees, round-trip travel distance and cost per unit distance, hired
  help, minimum call-out charge, and an optional discount.
- Returns: hours on site, price to quote, total direct costs, profit after
  direct costs AND the owner's own time at break-even, margin %, effective
  hourly rate for that job, and a plain-English verdict.
- Warns explicitly when the work type does not match a rate card row.

SHEET 5 — Quote Log
- 60 rows: date, customer, work type, price, hours, effective hourly
  (calculated), won y/n, notes.
- Totals: quotes sent, quotes won, win rate, value won.

VERIFIED
- 98 formulas, pre-2007 functions only, so nothing can degrade to #NAME?.
- Every formula re-computed in Python by verify_quote_calculator.py.

NOT IN THIS PRODUCT — never imply any of these
- No invoicing, no invoice numbering, no VAT or sales-tax return.
- No customer database or CRM, no job history per customer.
- No scheduling, calendar or route planning.
- No photo handling, before/after reports or PDF quote generation.
- No e-signature or customer-facing quote document.
- No payroll, no subcontractor tracking beyond one total pay figure.
- No mobile app. No automatic pricing by postcode or region.
- No industry benchmark rates: the 17 pre-filled rates are illustrative
  starting points to be overwritten, NOT researched market rates. An email
  must never present them as what anyone should charge.
